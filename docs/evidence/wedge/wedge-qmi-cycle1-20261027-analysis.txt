### Selective-wedge refinement (device-reliability gate.log, 2026-10-27)

Their kill-guarded uqmi ticks DURING the silent window (18:15/18:17/18:20Z)
returned partial answers — the wedge is **subsystem-selective**:

| Modem service | State in wedge window | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| NAS (registration) | **ALIVE** — `reg=searching mode=online` | gate.log ×3 ticks |
| WDS (bearer) | **ALIVE** — `wan=1`, bearer IP assigned | gate.log ×3 ticks |
| DMS (device mgmt) | dead (gate modem-check FAIL) | gate.log |
| UIM (SIM access / lpac) | dead | lpac silence post-enable |
| AT parser (all 4 ports) | dead | at_func_port empty ×3 probes |

The baseband control plane lived and even held a WAN bearer while the
UIM/DMS/AT stack was hung — **localizing the hang to the USIM-handling
subsystem** (dossier §6 H4 strengthened; whole-AP-dead models weakened).
`reg=searching` shows NAS actively scanning mid-window (USIM re-init churn
or carrier outage — the outage confounds the registration reading, not the
liveness reading).

**Wedge-depth scale (added to the harvest protocol):** on any future
silent-but-on-bus event, probe NAS and WDS alongside AT/UIM before
recovery — CTL-alive/UIM-dead = this class; nothing-alive = deeper. Cheap,
one uqmi call each, already in the gate's repertoire.

**Honest caveat + recovery-as-measurement:** persistence was 7 min at
DEFAULT-STOP; "would it have self-recovered at minute 12?" is unverified.
The owner's recovery is a free experiment: **reprobe immediately before
the cut and record the cut time** — pre-cut silence at 15+ min upgrades

extended-settle variant (which would itself amend the ladder's rung-1

## Consequences (pre-registered verdicts, applied)

1. **H_switch REJECTED.** Profile switching via the native-QMI backend is
   NOT reliably wedge-free on AACR13A03: fresh boot, zero prior ops,
   single enable → wedge. The 2026-10-26 18:44Z clean enable (dossier C3)
   was probabilistic luck, not fresh-boot protection.
2. **Reset-debt model amended:** fresh boot does not immunize the QMI
   path. Debt-free ≠ safe; the defect fires on the switch op itself
   across every transport tested (AT+CSIM ×3, QMI ×1).
3. **Firmware track (#38) returns to critical** — now with a captured
   specimen class: internal modem-software hang, host power state clean,
   USB function alive, command surfaces dead (H2/H4 in dossier §6;
   H1's power-domain latch remains the W1-class explanation only).
4. Card state at recovery: the enable completed card-side (`code:0`) —
   test/travel profile is ENABLED on the eUICC; silent.link displaced.
   Restoration (re-enable silent.link) after recovery, owner-directed.
